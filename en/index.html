WHAT IS PANNA?

Panna is a pure 1v1 football format that strips away everything except raw skill. Two players. One ball. No teamwork required.

The "panna" — putting the ball through your opponent's legs — is the signature move that defines the format. It's about creativity, technical ability, and mental toughness.

Panna League brings this authentic street football format to Lausanne with a championship structure, professional organization, and the reach to scale it across Switzerland.

  • ✓ Small-sided 1v1 football
  • ✓ Focused on skill & technique
  • ✓ Quick, intense matches
  • ✓ Tournament championship format
