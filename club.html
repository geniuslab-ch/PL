WHAT IS PANNA?

Panna is a small-sided football format focused on 1v1 skill, speed, technique and creativity. Players compete on a compact pitch with an emphasis on individual ability.

One of its signature moments is executing a "panna" — putting the ball through an opponent's legs — a move that showcases technical ability and decision-making in real time.

It's authentic football. No complicated rules. Just skill and competition.

WHAT WE NEED FROM A PARTNER

Suitable Space

A pitch, court or area suitable for small-sided 1v1 football (indoor or outdoor). The pilot doesn't require anything elaborate.

Football/Venue Access

Availability for a single event date. We'll work with you to find a time that suits your schedule.

Community Support

Share the player registration page with your players, academy members, or community. Help us build interest.

Logistical Collaboration

Basic coordination around event setup, registration and player arrival. We handle the rest.

The first pilot is intentionally simple. We're looking to collaborate and co-create, not simply sell something to you.
