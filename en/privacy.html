How we handle your information

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you register as a player or inquire about partnership opportunities, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Age group
  • Football club affiliation (optional)
  • Social media handle (optional)
  • How you heard about us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Contact you about Panna League events
  • Manage your registration
  • Send event updates and announcements
  • Respond to partnership inquiries
  • Improve our website and services

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We only retain information necessary to fulfill our purposes and comply with legal requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to store and manage your information, including:

  • Google Sheets / Airtable (data storage)
  • Google Analytics (website analytics)
  • Meta Pixel / TikTok Pixel (advertising)
  • Email service providers

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of communications

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at:

Email: pannaleague@mycountryisgoodat.com
Location: Lausanne, Switzerland

7. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

We comply with GDPR regulations. Your consent is required for communications. You may withdraw consent at any time by replying to any email with "unsubscribe".

This is a simple privacy policy for the MVP. As the service grows, this will be updated to reflect any changes in data handling practices.
